# Meme Candidate Scoring

A conservative research score for newly observed meme tokens.

Inputs cover liquidity, 24h volume, holder breadth, top-holder concentration, buy/sell flow, historical smart-money confidence, and FOMO intensity. The score is explicitly a WATCH/RESEARCH ranking, not a guaranteed buy signal.

High concentration and weak liquidity increase risk. Real production values must come from verified market/on-chain data; no synthetic observations should be inserted.
